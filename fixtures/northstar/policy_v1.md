# Northstar Outfitters refund policy (v1)

SYNTHETIC. Northstar Outfitters is a fictional outdoor-gear store written for EvalForge. These rules are not Stripe rules and not any real company's policy.

## NS-R1 Return window
Unworn, unwashed items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Values: 30 days

## NS-R2 Worn or used items
Items that show wear or use cannot be returned, unless they are defective (see NS-R3).
Values: none

## NS-R3 Defective items
Items with a manufacturing defect can be returned within 1 year of delivery for a replacement or a full refund, at the customer's choice.
Values: 1 year

## NS-R4 Refund timing
Northstar issues the refund within 5 business days after the returned item arrives at the warehouse.
Values: 5 business days

## NS-R5 Return shipping
Return shipping is free for defective items. For all other returns, a $7 return label fee is deducted from the refund.
Values: $7

## NS-R6 Final sale
Items marked "final sale" cannot be returned or refunded.
Values: none

## NS-R7 Gifts
Gift returns are refunded as Northstar store credit only, never to the buyer's card.
Values: none

## NS-R8 Original shipping charges
Original shipping charges are not refunded, unless the item is defective.
Values: none

## NS-R9 Damaged by the customer
If a returned item is missing parts or was damaged by the customer, up to 50% of the item price may be deducted from the refund.
Values: 50%

## NS-R10 Refund method
Refunds go to the original payment method. Store credit is available instead on request.
Values: none

## NS-R11 Cancelled orders
Orders cancelled before they ship get a full refund with no fee.
Values: none

## NS-R12 Price drops
If an item's price drops within 14 days of purchase, Northstar refunds the difference once per item.
Values: 14 days
